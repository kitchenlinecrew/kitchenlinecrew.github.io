Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how Kitchen Line Crew ("we", "us") handles information on this website, in our Etsy shop and on our Facebook and Pinterest accounts.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it has no sign-up forms. We do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information here.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log basic technical data such as your IP address when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Orders from our Etsy shop

When you buy from our Etsy shop, Etsy handles checkout and payment; we never see your card details. Etsy shares your name and shipping address with us so the order can be made and shipped. We pass those details only to our print partner, Printify, and its US print provider, who print and ship your order. We use them for nothing else. Etsy's own privacy policy covers everything you do on Etsy.

Our Facebook and Pinterest apps

We use the Facebook Graph API and the Pinterest API only to publish posts and pins to our own Facebook Page and Pinterest boards. Our apps do not log in visitors, do not read your profile, and do not collect, store or share data about people who view, like or comment on our posts.

Comments and messages you leave on Facebook or Pinterest are handled by those platforms under their own privacy policies.

Affiliate links

Some links go to retailers such as Selkirk. When you click one, the retailer or its affiliate network may use cookies to credit us with a referral. We never see what you buy or any of your payment details.

Data deletion

Our Facebook and Pinterest apps store no personal data, so there is nothing for them to delete. If you would like us to delete order details we hold from an Etsy purchase, remove a comment, or confirm that we hold nothing about you, message our Facebook page and we will respond within 30 days. You can also remove our app's access at any time in your Facebook or Pinterest settings.

Children

This site and our pages are meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions? Please message our Facebook page.
