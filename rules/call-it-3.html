CALL IT! #3

Paddle over the net?

The ball bounces near the net. You hit it on your side, and your follow-through carries your paddle over the net.

What's the call?

  • FAULT
  • NO FAULT
Show the call

NO FAULT

You may cross the plane of the net right after you hit the ball, as part of that same stroke. Just never touch the net or their court.

Rule 13.I.1

A player may cross the plane of the net immediately after hitting the ball. The allowance to cross the plane of the net only applies while executing the current strike of the ball and for continuation associated with the current strike of the ball.

From the 2026 USA Pickleball Official Rulebook. Read the official rulebook. Kitchen Line Crew is not affiliated with USA Pickleball.
